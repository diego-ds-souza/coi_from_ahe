Method

How the numbers were made

  1. Each trimmed library was mapped to its own MitoFinder contigs where one of them carries COX1, otherwise to its own COI from the pipeline. Only specimens with neither use the shared reference.
  2. Reads aligned over less than 80% of their length were dropped. Under the permissive settings of the pipeline's reference mapping, most reads in the COX1 window are short partial hits from elsewhere in the genome. In the specimen checked in detail, they tripled the apparent depth.
  3. The mapped reads were subsampled to 0.5%, 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 35%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the library. Duplicates were re-marked within each subsample, and the mean unique depth across COX1 was measured (07_rarefaction.sh).
  4. Unique against total mitochondrial reads was fitted with a gamma-Poisson saturation curve, which estimates how many distinct mitochondrial molecules each library holds (lib/rarefaction_model.py).
  5. MitoFinder's result for every specimen (step 06: complete means all 13 protein-coding genes and both rRNAs) was regressed on unique COX1 depth with a logistic model. The depth for the chosen chance of success is the target.
  6. For each specimen, the target depth was run back through its fitted curve to the mitochondrial reads needed, then divided by the library's mitochondrial fraction and fastp retention to give raw read pairs.

Read the estimates with these limits in mind

  • COX1 depth stands in for depth across the whole mitogenome. Mitochondrial genes share one copy number, but the AT-rich control region usually has lower coverage.
  • The calibration is observational: it says what depth complete assemblies had, across these libraries. Subsampling a few complete specimens and rerunning MitoFinder would test the threshold directly.
  • Projections assume more sequencing of the same library. A library past its ceiling needs a new library, or a re-amplification of the existing one, which this model cannot predict.
  • Where a library showed almost no duplicates, its ceiling is not bounded by the data and the projection is optimistic.
